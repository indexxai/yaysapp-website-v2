Account control

Delete your YaysApp account.

Permanently delete your account and associated YaysApp data. This action cannot be undone.

What happens when you delete

Your YaysApp profile, chat list, contacts, group and community memberships, starred messages, block list and devices are removed, and you are signed out everywhere. You will lose access to your account. Messages you already sent stay with the people who received them, shown as from “Deleted account”. Your Indexx / Bitcoin Yay account is separate and is deleted from that app.

Before deleting:
  • Complete or resolve any pending transactions or support cases.
  • Save anything you need from your account.
  • Cancel any separately billed subscription through the provider that bills you.

How to delete

In the YaysApp app (iPhone or Android): open Me › Profile and account › Delete account. We send a 6-digit code to your phone or email to confirm it is you, then your account is deleted.

On the web: sign in at web.yaysapp.com, open Settings › Profile and account › Delete account and confirm with the code.

Can’t sign in?

If you no longer have the phone number or email on your account, email privacy@bitcoinyay.com with the subject “YaysApp account deletion request” and your username. We will verify ownership before deleting the account.

Limited retention

Some records may be retained when required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial recordkeeping. Content already shared with other users may remain in their copy of a conversation, and public blockchain records cannot be erased. See our Privacy Policy for details.
