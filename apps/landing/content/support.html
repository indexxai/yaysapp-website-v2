Common questions

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Confirm you are using the same method used at registration—email and password, Google, or Apple. Use the password-recovery flow in the app for email accounts.

How do I control notifications?

Open YaysApp settings to adjust notification preferences, or use your device’s notification settings.

How do I delete my account?

Use Profile → Settings → Delete account in YaysApp or visit our account deletion page.

Should I send my wallet seed phrase to support?

No. YaysApp support will never ask for your password, OTP, private key, or seed phrase.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Read the public YaysApp Privacy Policy.
